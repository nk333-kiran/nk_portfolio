YAAR Foundation — Responsive Multi-Section Landing Website

📘 Overview — YAAR Foundation Website

YAAR Foundation is a responsive, multi-section landing website that showcases how YAAR empowers students, MSMEs, SHGs, tribal communities, and rural India through training, internships, marketplace support, and community programs.

The homepage acts as a visual story of the organisation, featuring a full-screen hero slider with different missions, clear navigation to About, Services, SHG Promotions, Gallery, Contact, and sections for Mission, Explore YAAR, Impact, Partners, Recent Activities, Testimonials, What’s New, CTA & Contact. It’s designed to feel modern, friendly, and impact-driven.

⚙️ Core Concept

The core concept of this YAAR site is:

“Use a single, dynamic landing page to communicate impact, opportunities, and pathways for empowerment.”

Key ideas reflected in the UI:

  • Storytelling with visuals — hero slides for Welcome, Empowering Rural India, Sustainable Livelihoods, Preserving Traditions, Robotics for Good.
  • Guided navigation — “Explore YAAR” cards help different users (shopper, SHG, student, community member) find their path.
  • Trust & impact — counters, testimonials, and activities show real reach.
  • Smooth experience — animations, hover effects, sliders, and scroll reveals keep the page engaging without feeling heavy.

🧠 Working Process (from a user’s point of view)

1️⃣ Landing & First Impression

  • User lands on the hero slider with animated particles and text.
  • They see what YAAR stands for: empowerment, innovation, rural upliftment, livelihoods, robotics, and SHGs.
  • Primary CTAs include: About YAAR, Explore Programs, Explore Shop, SHG Promotions, Services, Contact Us.

2️⃣ Understanding the Mission

  • Scrolling down, they reach the Mission section.
  • Clear mission statement: equitable growth through learning, maker culture, and market access for every learner, MSME, and SHG.
  • Bullet points highlight courses, SHG marketplace, internships & mentorship.
  • Buttons encourage them to Start Exploring or See Stories.

3️⃣ Choosing a Path (Quick Links)

In the “Explore YAAR” section, users choose what they want to do:

  • 🛍️ Visit our Shop → goes to yaar.shop to browse products.
  • 🧵 SHG Promotions → view SHG & company products and promotions.
  • 💼 Internships → student-facing, real-project based learning opportunities.
  • 👥 Community → events, maker clubs, and peer support.

Each card has a subtle 3D tilt effect and hover shadow, giving a premium, interactive feel.

4️⃣ Seeing Impact & Trust

  • Impact counters show live-animated stats such as Students Trained, MSMEs Supported, SHGs Empowered.
  • A partners marquee scrolls through labels like NGO Partners, Govt Schemes, Robotics Labs, Mentor Network, Community.
  • A Recent Activities slider highlights events like workshops, artisan fairs, youth training, health camps, and digital literacy drives.
  • Testimonials from the General Manager, Managing Director, and Brand Manager add credibility and human stories.

5️⃣ Latest Highlights & CTA

  • The What’s New slider shows rotating banners for Robotics Kits, MSME Courses, SHG Products.
  • The CTA + Contact section presents “Join Our Mission Today” and includes a contact form with fields for name, email, and interest (MSME Training / SHG Marketplace / Internships / Community).
  • Users can submit the form to receive follow-up from YAAR.

6️⃣ Connect & Follow

  • The footer includes quick links to Internship, SHG Promotions, Services, Reviews.
  • Social icons link to Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, WhatsApp, Email, Maps.
  • Floating WhatsApp and Call buttons enable instant communication.
  • A Back to Top button provides quick navigation back to the hero section.

🔍 Technologies Used

Markup & Structure

HTML5 for semantic structure and page layout.

Styling & Layout

Tailwind CSS (via CDN) plus custom CSS utilities for spacing, glassmorphism, and animations.

Fonts & Icons

Google Fonts – Inter for typography and Remix Icon for vector icons.

Hero & Carousels

Swiper.js for the hero slider, testimonials, and “What’s New” sections.

3D & Visual Effects

VanillaTilt.js for 3D card effects and particles.js for animated hero background.

Scroll & Counters

IntersectionObserver and custom JavaScript for reveal animations and animated counters.

Internationalisation

Google Translate widget integrated with a custom language dropdown for multi-language support.

Interactivity & Logic

Vanilla JavaScript for menus, sliders, buttons, scroll behaviour, and user interactions.

🌈 Example Output

Typical visitor flow on the site:

A visitor lands on the page and sees:
“Welcome to YAAR Foundation – Transforming lives through empowerment and innovation.”

They scroll to:
“Our Mission – To enable equitable growth by bringing industry-grade learning, maker culture, and market access to every learner, MSME and SHG.”

Then they click ‘Explore YAAR’ and choose for example:
• Visit our Shop → goes to YAAR Shop to support MSME & SHG products
• Internships → finds real-world learning opportunities

Meanwhile, impact counters animate:
“500+ Students Trained · 20+ MSMEs Supported · 30+ SHGs Empowered”

Finally, they submit the contact form under “Join Our Mission Today” with their interest.
